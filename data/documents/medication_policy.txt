MEDICATION POLICY: Acme Health Insurance
==========================================
Document ID: POL-2025-MED-001
Effective Date: January 1, 2025

SECTION 1: GENERAL COVERAGE RULES
-----------------------------------
1.1  All prescription medications must be prescribed by a licensed physician.
1.2  Generic medications are covered at 90% of the cost.
1.3  Brand-name medications are covered at 70% of the cost.
1.4  Over-the-counter (OTC) medications are NOT covered.

SECTION 2: DOSAGE LIMITS
--------------------------
2.1  The maximum covered dosage of Atorvastatin (Lipitor) is 80 mg per day.
2.2  The maximum covered dosage of Metformin is 2000 mg per day.
2.3  The maximum covered dosage of Ibuprofen is 1200 mg per day.
2.4  For patients under 18 years of age, the maximum covered dosage of 
     Ibuprofen is reduced to 600 mg per day.
2.5  For patients under 12 years of age, Atorvastatin is NOT covered.

SECTION 3: PRIOR AUTHORIZATION
--------------------------------
3.1  Medications costing more than $500 per month require prior authorization.
3.2  Specialty medications always require prior authorization regardless of cost.
3.3  Prior authorization requests must be submitted at least 14 days before 
     the prescription fill date.
3.4  Prior authorization is valid for 12 months from the approval date.

SECTION 4: REFILL POLICY
--------------------------
4.1  Standard prescriptions may be refilled up to 5 times.
4.2  Controlled substances (Schedule II-V) may be refilled up to 3 times.
4.3  Refills must be requested no earlier than 7 days before the current 
     supply runs out.
4.4  Mail-order prescriptions allow a 90-day supply per fill.
